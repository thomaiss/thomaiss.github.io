Road Game Innit

Project Date: November, 2023

Road Game innit is the first game that I ever created. It was made using Pygame it took me around 30 hours or so to create. The game is about you playing as a mail van, where you have to collect the parcels and dodge the traffic cones. Every parcel you successfully collect increases your score by 1, and if you hit a traffic cone then it's game over! The traffic cones in this game were part of the inspiration for my online avatar.

You can download the game on your computer if you'd like to play it yourself.
